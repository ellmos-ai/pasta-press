# AI Act Note (EU AI Act — informational)

**What PastaPress is:** a local command-line *component* that sends text to an
LLM instance operated by the user (Ollama) for stylistic refinement and
translation. It is not an AI system with its own model, and it is not offered
as a hosted service.

**Provider role:** the repository publishes source code under an open-source
license. Anyone deploying it operates their own local model and is responsible
for their deployment context.

**Intended purpose (limited):** stylistic text refinement and translation on
the user's own machines and texts. The default `gleichwertig` style
paraphrases at a comparable semantic, informational, and linguistic level and
may thereby reduce statistical text-watermark signals in token or phrasing
choices. This is a probabilistic effect, not a detection or removal guarantee.
Because the LLM generates new text, PastaPress also cannot guarantee that every
source detail is retained; users must review important output against the
source.

**Technical boundary:** PastaPress does not scan for or remove literal or
invisible Unicode signs, embedded marker strings, C2PA data, EXIF/XMP fields,
or other file/container metadata. Its deterministic reconstruction covers
chunk delimiters and boundary whitespace, not the contents generated by the
model inside each chunk.

**Not intended for:** any high-risk context listed in Annex III of the EU AI
Act (e.g. employment decisions, credit scoring, law enforcement, education
assessment), deception about authorship where disclosure is required, or
circumvention of mandatory transparency obligations. Reducing statistical
text patterns does not alter the origin or disclosure status of a text:
declare the AI share wherever disclosure is required or expected.

**Transparency (Art. 50):** output of PastaPress is AI-processed text. Users
who publish such text remain responsible for any applicable disclosure
obligations in their domain.
